# Privacy Policy

**Made For You CV**
Effective date: 2 October 2026

## Summary

This plugin does not collect, store or send your data anywhere. It is skills, scripts and templates only.

## What the plugin is

Made For You CV is a set of instructions (a skill), small scripts and HTML templates that run inside your own Claude session. It has no server, no backend, no analytics and no tracking.

## Your CV and job descriptions

The CV, job descriptions and other details you share are processed only inside your own Claude session. The plugin itself collects, stores and transmits nothing.

## Files it creates

Files generated during your session (such as `master_cv.md`, PDFs and `.txt` copies) are created in your own session. They are yours to keep or delete.

## Sensitive details

The plugin never puts national ID numbers or full street addresses on a CV.

## Claude and Anthropic

How Claude itself handles your data is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
