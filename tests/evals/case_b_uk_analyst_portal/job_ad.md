# Senior Data Analyst, Customer Insight at Pennine Mutual Insurance plc

Pennine Mutual is one of the UK's largest insurers, with 6,000 colleagues and over 4 million customers. Our Customer Insight team turns data into decisions across pricing, claims and retention.

**Location:** Leeds or Manchester (hybrid)
**Reference:** PM-24871

## About the role
As a Senior Data Analyst you will lead analysis for our retention and claims teams, mentor junior analysts and present findings to senior stakeholders.

## Essential
- Significant experience as a data analyst in a large organisation
- Advanced SQL
- Experience building dashboards in Tableau or Power BI
- Experience presenting insights to non-technical stakeholders
- Strong attention to data quality and governance

## Desirable
- Python or R for analysis and automation
- Experience with Azure Synapse or another cloud data warehouse
- Insurance or financial services experience
- Experience mentoring colleagues
