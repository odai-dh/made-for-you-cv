# Frontend Developer at Brightwell Health AB

Brightwell Health builds patient-facing web apps for Swedish clinics. We are 25 people in Gothenburg and Stockholm and we ship every week.

**Location:** Stockholm (hybrid, 2 days in the office)

## What you will do
- Build accessible patient and clinic interfaces in React and TypeScript
- Work closely with designers and a backend team
- Write unit and end-to-end tests and review colleagues' code

## You have
- 1+ years of professional experience with React and TypeScript
- Knowledge of accessibility (WCAG) and a habit of testing with real users and tools
- Experience with automated testing (unit tests)
- Good English and Swedish

## Nice to have
- Cypress or Playwright for end-to-end tests
- Experience with Next.js
- Interest in healthcare or regulated software
- Experience with design systems
